# DualEntry – Hardcore Engineer Role Summary

## Direct Answer
DualEntry is hiring ambitious software engineers at all experience levels for a high-intensity, fully remote position, offering **$200K–$400K base salary** plus **$75K–$175K equity**. The role prioritizes work ethic and ownership over credentials or seniority.

---

## Key Facts

- **Company**: DualEntry — an AI-focused fintech startup, ~18 months old
- **Funding**: $100M+ raised from Lightspeed, Khosla, Contrary, and Google Ventures
- **Role**: Hardcore Engineer (All Levels) — Engineering department
- **Employment**: Full-time, fully remote
- **Regions**: EMEA, EU, LATAM, NAMER, UK, Nordics, and India

### Compensation
- 💰 Base: **$200,000 – $400,000 USD**
- 📈 Equity: **$75,000 – $175,000 USD**
- 🗽 Visa sponsorship available for NYC relocation after 2+ years

### What They Look For
- **High agency and full ownership** of work
- **Outlier work ethic** — explicitly stated as 9am–9pm, 6 days/week
- Obsession with building and shipping **extremely fast**
- Language-agnostic — fast learning is valued over specific stack knowledge
- Ambition prioritized over experience level
