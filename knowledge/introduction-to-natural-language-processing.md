## Summary
# Natural Language Processing (NLP)

## Direct Answer
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of artificial intelligence (AI) that enables computers to understand, interpret, generate, and interact with human language in a meaningful way.

---

## Key Facts
- **Interdisciplinary field**: NLP combines linguistics, computer science, and machine learning
- **Core goal**: Bridge the gap between human communication and computer understanding
- **Input types**: Works with text and/or speech data
- **Subfield of AI**: Closely related to machine learning and deep learning

---

## Core Tasks & Applications
- **Text Classification** – Categorizing text (e.g., spam detection, sentiment analysis)
- **Machine Translation** – Translating between languages (e.g., Google Translate)
- **Named Entity Recognition (NER)** – Identifying names, places, dates in text
- **Speech Recognition** – Converting spoken language to text
- **Text Summarization** – Condensing long documents into shorter versions
- **Question Answering** – Systems like chatbots and virtual assistants (e.g., Siri, ChatGPT)
- **Part-of-Speech Tagging** – Identifying grammatical roles of words

---

## Key Concepts
- **Tokenization**: Breaking text into individual words or phrases
- **Stemming/Lemmatization**: Reducing words to their root forms
- **Word Embeddings**: Representing words as numerical vectors (e.g., Word2Vec, GloVe)
- **Transformers**: Modern neural network architecture powering advanced NLP models (e.g., BERT, GPT)

---

## Common Challenges
- Ambiguity and context-dependence of language
- Sarcasm, idioms, and figurative speech
- Handling multiple languages and dialects
- Bias present in training data

---

## Real-World Examples
| Application | Example |
|---|---|
| Virtual Assistants | Alexa, Siri, Google Assistant |
| Search Engines | Google Search query understanding |
| Healthcare | Clinical note analysis |
| Finance | Sentiment analysis of news for trading |

---

## Summary
NLP allows machines to process and understand human language, powering everyday technologies from search engines to chatbots. Modern NLP relies heavily on **deep learning** and **transformer-based models**, making it one of the fastest-growing areas in AI today.
